# ProcessPatch — research grant procedure demo policies

## Policy V1 (2026-08-01, POLICY-V1)

### Sec 4.1 Eligibility threshold
Applicants must have CGPA >= 8.0.

### Sec 4.2 Recommendation
All applicants must provide a faculty recommendation.

### Sec 4.3 Ordering / prerequisite
Department approval must occur before final submission.
