# Employee reimbursement policy V2 (2026-09-18, REIMB-V2) — amends REIMB-V1

### Sec 2.1 Claim limit (tightened)
Claims up to amount <= 50000 are accepted without extra review. Larger claims need finance review.

### Sec 2.2 Approval (conditional)
Manager approval is required only when amount > 50000.

### Sec 2.3 Ordering (unchanged)
Payment must occur after verification.

### Sec 2.4 Deadline (new)
Claims MUST NOT be submitted after 2026-09-30.
