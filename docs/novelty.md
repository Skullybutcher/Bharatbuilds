# Novelty

We do not claim policy-to-code, workflow graphs, legal-change impact, semantic
diff witnesses, rule-driven testing, or counterexample-guided repair as
individually novel (see research references in the spec: Rules as Code,
Prose2Policy, PolicyGuide, LegalChanges4BPC, ADDiff, Orvalho et al. AAAI 2025).

Our experiment is their combination as a witness-first repair loop for
policy-driven human procedures, plus evidence-backed impact quantification and
hash-bound human governance before any procedure changes.
